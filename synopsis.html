Sentiment Sense

AI-led employee pulse surveys over Telegram, with HR-ready reports.

Problem

Employee pulse surveys are either too rigid (form fatigue, low signal) or too costly to run conversationally at scale. HR teams want honest, contextual feedback without scheduling 1:1s with every employee or relying on raw star ratings, and want to act on what they hear without wading through transcripts.

Solution

Sentiment Sense runs short, AI-led check-ins with employees on a channel they already use: Telegram. Each conversation is summarised into a structured report (sentiment, themes, quotes, suggested follow-up). HR can mix custom questions with AI-generated ones per wave, track per-survey AI cost, and review reports on a single dashboard.

How it works

1Importemployees via CSV or form
2Onboardshare Telegram deep link
3Open chatbot greets the employee
4ConverseLLM-driven Q&A turns
5Reportsummary, themes, quotes
6ReviewHR acts on dashboard

What HR gets

  • Configurable surveys. For each survey, HR sets how many questions the bot asks (e.g. 10 or 20) and the split between custom questions HR provides and ones the AI generates (e.g. 50% custom).
  • Triage by sentiment. Each report gets a 0-100 sentiment score and Red / Yellow / Green band so HR can sort reds first. Open / Reviewed / Resolved / Dismissed states with HR notes; full transcript visible alongside.
  • Grounded reports. LLM constrained to the actual transcript and stored employee profile, no fabricated specifics, no medical claims.
  • Cost transparency. Per-survey prompt/completion tokens and USD cost from OpenRouter shown inline on the dashboard.
  • Resilient delivery. Offset-confirmed Telegram polling with update-id deduping, so transient API errors never drop messages.

Survey lifecycle

Pending Active Completed Cancelled Failed

Pending until the employee opens their onboarding link; Active during the conversation; Completed when the bot wraps up cleanly. Failed captures Telegram or LLM errors at the finalize turn, with a placeholder report still saved so the transcript stays reviewable.

Bot behavior & safety

  • One turn at a time; opener is templated or the first custom question, follow-ups are LLM-driven within a question budget plus buffer.
  • Instructed to ask custom questions verbatim or with minor rewording, and not duplicate their topics in its own follow-ups.
  • Avoids medical/mental-health diagnosis and unnecessary sensitive personal questions; never promises HR action on the bot side.
Why it matters. HR gets richer, more contextual signal than a static survey, employees get a low-friction chat instead of yet another survey link, and the whole loop runs from CSV import to a reviewable, HR-ready report.
